# Chaosmos community

The place to talk about [Chaosmos](https://chaosmos.studio) puzzle games: Plumb, Carillon and Apsis.
Everything happens in [Discussions](https://github.com/chaosmos-studio/community/discussions).

| Category | For |
| --- | --- |
| Announcements | News from the studio: launches, updates, new levels |
| Comments | One thread for each page of chaosmos.studio, made the first time someone comments there |
| Q&A | Stuck on a level, or something not working? Ask here |
| Ideas | A rule, a level or a feature you would like to see |
| Show and tell | A solve you are proud of, a share card, a clever route |
| Polls | Votes on what comes next |
| General | Anything else |

## A few manners

- Be kind, to players and to the games.
- Keep solutions out of titles, and put them inside a spoiler, so nobody has a puzzle spoiled by accident:

  ```html
  <details><summary>Solution</summary>

  Up, left, down.

  </details>
  ```

- For a bug, say which game, its version (Settings shows it) and your phone.

## The games

- [Plumb](https://chaosmos.studio/games/plumb/): tilt the board, and every marble rolls at once.
- [Carillon](https://chaosmos.studio/games/carillon/): one tap, and the chain plays a tune.
- [Apsis](https://chaosmos.studio/games/apsis/): send every arrow out of orbit, in the right order.

Something private? Write to hello@chaosmos.studio.
